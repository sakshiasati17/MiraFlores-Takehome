# MiraFlores AI — Case Study Brief (AI Engineer, Tax)

_Plain-text copy of docs/brief.docx, the brief sent by MiraFlores AI. Wording unchanged._

We want to understand what problems you notice, what motivates you, and
how you turn an idea into a useful solution. Choose a problem from your
work, everyday life, or an industry you care about. It does not need to
involve accounting.

# Choose your problem

Briefly describe three problems you would like to solve. For each, tell
us where you encountered it, who experiences it, and what happens
because it remains unsolved. A few sentences per problem are enough.

Choose one to explore. Why does it interest you, and why should someone
else care about solving it? Explain why it deserves attention ahead of
the other two. Separate what you have observed from what you are
assuming.

# Develop your approach

Explain how people handle the problem today and what a better outcome
would mean. Propose a direction, then choose the smallest useful first
step. Discuss a credible alternative you considered and why you did not
choose it.

You decide where AI, conventional software, or a process change would
help. Show how the first step connects to the broader opportunity. What
would you need to learn, and what would make you decide the problem is
not worth solving?

# Build a working prototype

Build a working prototype of your proposed solution. It must complete
one useful core workflow from a realistic input to a meaningful output
or action that addresses your chosen problem. Choose a narrow scope you
can finish within the time limit. You may use a web app, command-line
tool, notebook, API, or another runnable format.

Demonstrate a representative use case and one difficult or invalid-input
case. The core problem-solving logic must actually run. Synthetic data
and mocked external integrations are allowed if clearly labeled. Static
screens, pseudocode, or a presentation alone do not satisfy the
prototype requirement.

# Time and submission

Spend 2–4 hours and stop at four hours. Submit a concise explanation of
up to four pages, your working prototype, and its source code. Include a
README with setup and run instructions, dependencies, sample data, and
example inputs and expected outputs so we can run it. Note the time
spent and what you would investigate next.

AI tools are allowed. Briefly state how you used AI, how you checked its
output, and which parts of any prior work you reused. Identify what is
implemented, mocked, or deferred. No paid service or production account
is required.

We will review your choices and watch your prototype run for 15 minutes,
then conduct the separate 30-minute live case. Be ready to walk through
the implementation, explain your decisions, and adapt when information
changes.
